I really enjoyed this. It doesn't feel like a story from the old Resident Evil games. It's closer to the Ethan Winters era, and that's fine. I actually think it's much better to do something tangential to Resident Evil than to try to replicate it. We already have the animated series for that, and the attempts with Alice and everything mostly didn't work. So it was really nice to finally see a good Resident Evil movie.

The first half is the stronger part. The ending is really abrupt, but it's quite fun too.

I love Zach Cregger as a director, and Austin Abrams is great as the lead. He was great as the junkie in Weapons and he's great in this too. I came away pretty happy with it, and pleasantly surprised, to be honest. A 7.5 for me.
