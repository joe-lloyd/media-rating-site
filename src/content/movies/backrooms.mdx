I thought this was going to be terrible. A movie based on a meme from a 4chan board didn't sound that promising, but it ended up doing some really interesting stuff.

What I liked most is that the Backrooms are mostly just the setting. They're almost incidental to the story. The characters are really well put together and the performances are fantastic. There's some fun humour in there too, while it still feels like a proper mainstream horror movie. The handycam stuff gives it some scary moments, and I thought they handled that well.

I also like the work Kane Parsons did fleshing out the Backrooms in his online series. It's cool that he got the chance to make this at such a young age. A really good effort, and I came away pretty happy with it. I was between a 7 and a 7.5, but I think 7 is right for me.
