Really enjoyed this one. Linda Cardellini, who played Velma in the Scooby-Doo movies, is one of my favourite actresses, and she's super good here as Pamela Voorhees.

I wasn't so keen on the need to explain everything and spell out how it all works. I don't need an answer for every bit of the Friday the 13th world. Even so, I thought this was really fun and interesting. I'm a fan of the films, so I got a lot out of spending more time in that world. A 7.5 for me.
