I didn't know what the fuck was going on most of the time, and I still really enjoyed it.

The story is a bit of a mess. Some of that might be on me, because it had been so long since I'd watched season 1 and seen these characters.

Even when I was lost, it was great to watch. The animation is amazing, like everyone says. The fight scenes are just the best, so well put together. The characters are super interesting.

I really enjoyed the feeling of the whole thing. It's really cool. An 8 from me.
