Resident Evil 4 Remake is excellent. CAPCOM did a great job modernising the original without losing what made it special — the pacing, the variety, the tone. It still feels like RE4 but plays like a modern game.

The combat feels great, the village, castle and island structure keeps things varied throughout, and there's enough content to keep you engaged all the way through. One of the better remakes in recent memory and a strong entry in the RE series.
