Separate Ways is a great addition to RE4 Remake. Playing as Ada gives you a different perspective on the same events and the campaign is tight and well paced for a DLC. It doesn't overstay its welcome.

Ada's playstyle feels distinct enough to justify the separate campaign, and the story threads it adds are genuinely interesting. If you enjoyed the main game it's worth playing through — it rounds out the full picture nicely.
